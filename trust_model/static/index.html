    Who trusts whom

    Every way of filling 5 seats from 5 strategies (126 team mixes) is simulated, giving a payoff table. From it: which "everyone plays X" teams are Nash equilibria (no single player gains by switching), and where replicator dynamics lead (strategies that earn more than average spread through a population of players). Payoff differences between strategies are only a few hundredths of a point, so small runs are noisy: use 20–40 games to explore, and 100+ before drawing conclusions.
